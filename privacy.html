Brainzo

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-19 · Nikhil Padwal

Brainzo is a brain-training puzzle game made by Nikhil Padwal ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how. The short version: Brainzo has no accounts and no servers of its own, and nothing you do in the game is sent to us. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, set up in a child-safe way (see section 4).

1. Summary

  • We do not collect, receive, or store any personal information ourselves.
  • There is no sign-up, login, or account.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as child-directed, limited to content suitable for all ages, and non-personalised. The app does not use the advertising ID.
  • There is no analytics or tracking software, and we do not sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, Brainzo saves the following in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share something (see section 5).

  • A display name, if you enter one. It is optional; a nickname works fine.
  • Your age group (for example "Teens 13–15"), chosen difficulty, text size, theme, sound, language, and reminder settings.
  • Game progress: levels, stars, points, streaks, lives, badges, puzzle statistics, and puzzles saved for review.
  • Your chosen avatar face and frame. These are built-in drawings; the app never uses your photos or camera.
  • The times of recent ad clicks, kept for one day, used only to pause ads if they look accidental or invalid (see section 4).

We cannot see, access, or recover any of this information.

3. Permissions the app may ask for

Each permission is optional and is only requested when you use the feature that needs it. The game works fully without them.

  • Notifications: for optional daily reminders to keep your streak. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no notification server is involved.
  • Vibration: for gentle feedback on taps and answers. You can turn it off in Settings.

Brainzo plays bundled music and sound effects, but it does not use or request the microphone and never records audio. It does not use the camera or your photos, and it does not access your location, contacts, or any account information.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Brainzo free, it shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a small banner on the home screen and level map, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and an optional ad you can choose to watch to earn a life. Ads never interrupt a puzzle: the only ad shown during a level is one you choose to watch for a life.

Brainzo is for players aged 13 and up. Because many of them are teenagers, we use the strictest ad settings and configure every ad request so that:

  • it is tagged as child-directed (treated under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (under the EU GDPR);
  • ads are limited to the "G" content rating, suitable for all audiences;
  • ads are non-personalised: they are not based on your interests or on activity in other apps;
  • the app does not request or send the Android advertising ID.

To deliver ads, measure them, and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends information to Google, such as your device model and operating system, the IP address (which can suggest an approximate location), app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. This information goes to Google, not to us; we only see total ad statistics that do not identify anyone.

Google's use of this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Invalid click protection: to stop accidental or invalid ad clicks, the app keeps a short record of ad click times on your device only. If there are 2 ad clicks within a minute, or 3 in a day, ads pause on that device for 24 hours. Banners are placed apart from game buttons and labelled "Advertisement". This record is never sent to us and is removed after a day, or when you uninstall the app.

Apart from the ads SDK, Brainzo includes no analytics, crash-reporting, or social-media SDKs, and makes no network requests to us.

5. Sharing you choose to do

Some features let you share a score or a friend challenge using your device's share menu. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share and choose an app. A challenge link contains the puzzle set number, your score, and your display name, so that your friend can see who challenged them. Once shared, the message is handled by the app you chose (for example a messaging app), under that app's own privacy policy.

If you would rather not share your name, use a nickname, or leave the name empty.

6. Children

Brainzo is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and children under 13 should not use it.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or from anyone else. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used the app, you can delete everything it stored by uninstalling it, and you are welcome to contact us using the details below.

Players aged 13 to 17 should use the app with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. We encourage them to use a nickname instead of their real name and to ask before sharing a challenge with someone. Ads are served under the child-safe settings described in section 4.

Parents and guardians can review everything the app stores by looking at the Settings and Progress screens, and can delete it at any time (see section 7). If you have a question about your child's use of the app, contact us using the details below.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Erase all progress and settings in Settings → Reset all progress.
  • Uninstall Brainzo, which removes all of its data from the device.

Because we never receive your information, we have nothing to delete on our side. Note that the app does not back up your progress, so uninstalling or changing devices starts a fresh game.

8. Security

Brainzo's data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and is protected by your device's own security (such as your screen lock).

9. Services provided by others

Brainzo is distributed through Google Play and shows ads from Google AdMob (section 4). Google, and your device's operating system, may collect information under their own privacy policies (for example, to provide app downloads, updates, ads, or system crash reports). We do not receive personal information from that collection.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as the right to access or delete it. Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete, but you are welcome to contact us with any request or question. For information handled by Google for ads, see https://policies.google.com/privacy, where Google explains how to exercise your rights.

11. Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how information is handled (for example online leaderboards), we will update this policy and the "Effective" date before those features are released, and ask for consent where the law requires it. The current policy is always available in the app under Settings → About.
